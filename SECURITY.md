# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@meritbox.com.au** (machine-readable contact: `/.well-known/security.txt`). Please include steps to reproduce and the impact. We will:

- acknowledge within 2 business days
- give an initial assessment within 5 business days
- keep you updated, and credit you (if you wish) once fixed

Please do not access other customers' data, degrade the service, or publicly disclose before a fix is released. Good-faith research under this policy won't face legal action from us.

## Scope

In scope: `*.meritbox.com.au`, the Meritbox application and API, and this repository.
Out of scope: denial-of-service, social engineering, physical attacks, and findings that only affect outdated browsers.

## Supported versions

Only the current production release receives security fixes.
